10. 시행일 및 변경

This service replies to comments on posts of the Instagram account @riri.devlog and delivers requested links via DM. Comment text and usernames are not stored. Instagram user IDs and comment IDs are used only while delivering the DM and are deleted as soon as delivery ends (at most 7 days). To prevent sending more than twice to the same user on the same post, an irreversible hashed value is kept for 7 days. Only anonymous counts are kept after that. Data is processed through Meta (Instagram) and Cloudflare, is never sold or shared, and deletion requests are honored at ssadaegi282@gmail.com.
